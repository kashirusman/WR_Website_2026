Spread 10 · The Handwriting — The Pivot
Team meeting notes, early 2009

— whiteboard, 2009
010
The shift that
changed everything

Until 2009, White Rice had been building excellent content. Beautiful, evidence-based, culturally considered. But content moves minds individually.

The pivot was this: the community is not the audience. The community is the author. When you hand the story back to the people it's about, it spreads because it's true — not because you amplified it.

This is the theory that would take White Rice from 400 schools to 50 million people.
